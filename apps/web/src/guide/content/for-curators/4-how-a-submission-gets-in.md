**What it is.** Two paths lead from the submissions space into the main catalog, and both run through trusted judgment.

**The curator path.** A curator reads a submission, judges it belongs, and chooses Promote. The catalog steward's process then writes the book into the catalog proper, where it behaves like any other book: it shows up in search and browse, and readers shelve it like anything else.

**The automatic path.** A submission that enough trusted readers rate well crosses the threshold on its own, with no one needing to press anything. The bar is deliberately both a count and a quality floor: several trusted readers, rating it well. A crowd of strangers, or one enthusiast, never moves a book in.

**While it waits.** A submission is already ratable and taggable in the submissions space, so the judgment that promotes it accumulates in the open.
