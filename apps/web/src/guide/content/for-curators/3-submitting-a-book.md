**What it is.** The catalog is large and still missing things. "Submit a book" adds what you cannot find: you give the title, author, and what details you have, and the book joins the community submissions, visible and ratable while it waits.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose "Submit a book" from the menu.
2. Enter the title and author. If the book is widely known, the form offers to fill the rest (cover, year, pages) for you to check.
3. If you wrote the book yourself, say so with the toggle at the bottom; it records you as the author on the record.
4. Submit. The book is now in the submissions space, and the path into the main catalog is described in the next entry.
