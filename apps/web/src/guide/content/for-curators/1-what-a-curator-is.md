**What it is.** A curator is a reader whose judgment carries weight here: their ratings shape the house view, their word counts on classifications, and they hold the catalog tools described in this section. The role recognizes taste that other trusted people stand behind.

**The three ways in.** Some curators were invited when the site began. Some are vouched in: enough trusted people put their own names behind a reader's judgment. Some earn the standing on their record alone, as the network comes to trust them through their work. All three arrive at the same role with the same tools.

**What it asks of you.** Care, mostly. The tools in this section act on a catalog every reader shares, and each action is recorded under your name.
