**What it is.** The Curate link in the menu, shown to curators, leads to the community submissions: the books waiting for judgment, each with its trust signals (how many trusted readers rated it, and how well).

**How to use it.**

1. Choose Curate from the menu.
2. Read the waiting submissions; the signals beside each are decision support, never a decision.
3. Promote what belongs. The catalog is what curators make it.
