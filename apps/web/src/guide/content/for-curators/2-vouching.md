**What it is.** Putting your name behind another reader's judgment. "Vouch as curator" on a profile records that you believe this person's taste deserves standing, and the profile shows how many trusted people agree.

**What it is based on.** A vouch only counts from someone the network already trusts, and one vouch is never enough on its own. The circle grows the way reputations grow anywhere: several people who are themselves trusted, each deciding independently.

**How to use it.**

1. Open the profile of the reader you want to vouch for.
2. Choose "Vouch as curator." The count under their name updates.
3. To take a vouch back, the same control withdraws it. A withdrawn vouch stops counting, the same as it started.
