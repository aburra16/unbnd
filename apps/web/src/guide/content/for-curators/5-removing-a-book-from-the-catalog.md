**What it is.** The undo for a promotion that turned out to be wrong: junk that slipped through, a duplicate, a misrepresented submission. "Remove from catalog" appears to curators on community-added books.

**How to use it.**

1. Open the book and choose "Remove from catalog."
2. Confirm. The action is deliberate on purpose; it takes a book out of every reader's catalog.
3. The page shows "Removal queued" while the catalog steward's process carries it out, and the submissions list shows the same state.

**What removal means.** The book leaves search, browse, and the shelves' displays, and returns to the community submissions space. Nothing written about it is destroyed: the ratings, tags, and shelf entries readers made are all kept, and if the book is later promoted again, everything comes back with it.
