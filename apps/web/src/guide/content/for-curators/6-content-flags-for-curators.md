**What it is.** The curator's side of the reviewed signals readers see. Curators can raise a serious concern about a book, see concerns that are still pending, and decide whether a substantiated concern is shown.

**How it works, end to end.**

1. Raising. The tag picker offers the serious flags, like "AI generated," only to curators. A raised flag is recorded but hidden from readers.
2. Pending. On the book page, curators see the hidden flag with how many trusted people raised it: the substance behind the concern, visible before any decision.
3. Showing. A curator who judges the concern substantiated chooses Reveal. The flag then appears to readers as a reviewed signal, attributed to that review.
4. Withdrawing. The same control withdraws a shown flag, and it leaves the page the way it arrived: by a recorded decision.

**The weight of it.** A flag is an accusation against someone's book. The product gates every step of this to curators, keeps the pending state invisible to readers, and records who decided what. Treat it with the gravity the design does.
