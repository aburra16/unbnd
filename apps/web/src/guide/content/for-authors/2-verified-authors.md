**What it is.** A claim becomes verification when enough trusted readers stand behind it. A verified author's name carries the verified mark, and the author gains something concrete: the right to speak on their own book's page.

**What it is based on.** Trusted readers who know the claim to be true vouch for it. Verification needs more than one voucher, so no single person, including the author, can confer it.

**What it unlocks.** A verified author can provide their book's description, cover, and where-to-buy link. Anything the author provides is labeled "From the author" on the page, so readers always know which words are the author's own and which came from the catalog.
