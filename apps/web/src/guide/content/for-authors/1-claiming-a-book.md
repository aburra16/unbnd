**What it is.** If you wrote a book in the catalog, you can say so. "Claim this book" on the book page records your statement, and the book shows you among its claimants.

**What a claim is, plainly.** A claim is your word. It is public, recorded under your name, and visible to readers weighing it. It is a statement of authorship, and it stays a statement until trusted readers verify it; verification is its own, separately earned state.

**How to use it.**

1. Sign in and open the book you wrote.
2. Choose "Claim this book."
3. Your claim appears with the book. What happens next is described under Verified authors.
