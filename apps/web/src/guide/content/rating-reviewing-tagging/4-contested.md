**What it is.** A tag drawn with a line through it and a small "contested" label. It means trusted readers dispute this tag at least as much as they back it: a live disagreement, shown as one.

**Why it shows at all.** Hiding the tag would pretend the fight never happened, and presenting it as settled would overstate the consensus. The struck treatment is the honest middle: the tag exists, and the people whose judgment counts have not accepted it.

**What you can do.** Add your own voice. Apply the tag if you think it fits, or dispute it if you think it does not; contested tags resolve the same way they arose, by readers weighing in.
