**What it is.** Your own named collections: read, reading, want to read, or anything you invent. The "Shelves" area on every book page saves the book; your profile shows your shelves to anyone who visits.

**How to use it.**

1. Open the Shelves area on a book page.
2. Pick an existing shelf, or type a name to make a new one.
3. To take a book off a shelf, open the same area and remove it. The shelf keeps its other books.

Shelving is independent of rating: an unrated book can sit on a shelf, and a rated one never has to.
