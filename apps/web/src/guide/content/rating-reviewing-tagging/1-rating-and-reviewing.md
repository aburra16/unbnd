**What it is.** Your stars and your words on a book, published under your name. One rating per book, one to five stars, with an optional written review.

**How to use it.**

1. Open the book page and choose your stars in the rating area.
2. Add a review in the box below them if you have something to say.
3. Choose "Submit rating." A quiet line confirms it, and the date is recorded.

**Changing your mind.** Open the same book later and the control is prefilled with your rating. Change the stars or the words and choose "Update rating"; the new rating replaces the old one everywhere it shows. There is one current rating per book, always your latest.
