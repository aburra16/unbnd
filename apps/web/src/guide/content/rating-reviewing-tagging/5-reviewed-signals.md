**What it is.** A small area apart from the normal tags where serious concerns about a book are shown, with wording like "AI generated." A signal here is an accusation, and the site treats it with more care than a genre tag.

**What it is based on.** Concerns of this kind can only be raised by trusted readers, and a raised concern stays out of sight until it has been reviewed and deliberately made public by the site's catalog steward. What you see is the result: a concern with enough substance behind it that someone accountable chose to show it.

**How to read it.** The signal is attributed to that review, never presented as a community vote. If a shown signal is later withdrawn, it disappears the same way it arrived: by a deliberate, recorded decision.
