**What it is.** Taking a rating back entirely. A removed rating leaves the book's average, your profile counts, your taste matches, and the For You shelf, which can recommend the book again.

**How to use it.**

1. Open a book you have rated. Below the rating control, choose "Remove rating."
2. A short confirmation asks once. Choose "Remove," or "Keep it" to back out.
3. The rating is gone from every place it counted.

**Coming back.** There is no separate undo. Rate the book again and the new rating stands, the same as any other.
