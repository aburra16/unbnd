**What it is.** The "Classification" area on a book page: the genres and styles readers have applied, each chip carrying a small count of how many stand behind it. Anyone signed in can suggest a tag or dispute one, and the page shows the consensus that results.

**What it is based on.** Each chip's count is the number of readers applying that tag. The section's label tells you whose consensus you are reading: trusted readers when enough of them have weighed in, the whole community otherwise.

**How to use it.**

1. Open the Classification area on a book page and choose "Suggest a genre or style."
2. Pick the tag that fits. Your assertion counts once, and you can change your mind later.
3. To disagree with an applied tag, dispute it from the same picker. A dispute counts against the tag's consensus rather than deleting anything.
