**What it is.** The four moves that make the site start working for you. Each one takes a minute, and each one feeds the next.

**How to use it.**

1. Rate a few books you know well. Open a book page, choose the stars, and add a line of review if you have one. Your rating counts the moment you save it.
2. Save a book to a shelf. Every book page has a Shelves area; pick a shelf or make your own.
3. Follow a reader whose taste you respect. Open their profile and choose Follow. Following is how the site learns whose judgment matters to you.
4. Switch the view on a book you rated. The numbers change with the judges, and that switch is the heart of the site.
