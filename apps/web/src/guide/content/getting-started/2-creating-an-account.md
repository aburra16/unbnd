**What it is.** An account lets you rate, review, shelve, and follow. Unbnd offers two ways in, and the email path is the right choice for almost everyone.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose "Sign in" at the top right, then "Continue with email."
2. Pick a display name, enter your email, and choose a password.
3. That is the whole thing. You can rate your first book within the minute.

The second sign-in option is for readers who already hold a portable reading account from the wider network Unbnd is part of, and connect through a small browser extension. If that sentence describes you, the marked note in "Your account is yours" has the full story. If it does not, the email path gives you everything, including the option to take full ownership of your account later.
