**What it is.** A place to keep your reading life: the books you have read, what you thought of them, and the shelves you sort them onto. The part that is new is where the numbers come from. On most book sites, a rating is an average of everyone. Here, every number can be traced to people, and the site can show you the same book through different sets of eyes: the whole community, the readers this site's network trusts, or the people you follow.

**What that means for you.** Your ratings and reviews are published under your own name and stay yours. Nobody can edit them behind your back, and if you ever leave, you can take your account with you. The details live in the last section of this guide, "Your account is yours."

**How to start.**

1. Read the landing page of this guide for the short version.
2. Follow the steps in [Your first session](/guide/getting-started#your-first-session).
