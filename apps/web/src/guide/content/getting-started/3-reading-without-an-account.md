**What it is.** Everything readable on Unbnd is readable without an account: the catalog, every book page with its ratings and reviews, public profiles and shelves, and search. When a friend sends you a link, you see the whole page, with the ratings and the people behind them.

**Where the account starts.** The account question comes up at the moment you act, and only then. Choose a star rating, save a book to a shelf, or follow someone, and a small prompt offers to create your free account in place. Nothing is lost; the page you were reading stays right there.
