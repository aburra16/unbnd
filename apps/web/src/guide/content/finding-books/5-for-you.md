**What it is.** A signed-in shelf of recommendations picked for you, from books you have not rated yet.

**What it is based on.** Your own ratings and the people you follow. The more books you rate, the better it gets, and a book leaves the shelf the moment you rate it. If you remove a rating later, the book becomes recommendable again.

**How to use it.**

1. Sign in and rate a handful of books first; recommendations need something to work from.
2. Find the For You row on the homepage.
