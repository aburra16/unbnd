**What it is.** Everything readers have recorded about one book, top to bottom.

**What you will find, in order.**

1. The cover and the basics: author, year, pages, language, and the genre and style chips readers have applied.
2. The description, with its source named.
3. The ratings area: the average, the star spread, how many ratings stand behind it, and the faces of recent raters. A line above it names the view; a line below it may call the book a hidden gem or overhyped when trusted readers and the crowd disagree.
4. Reviews, with the reviewer's name and date, sortable by how trusted the reviewer is.
5. The rating control: your stars, your review, and the option to update or remove them later.
6. Classification: the genre and style consensus, with the option to suggest or dispute a tag.
7. Shelves: save the book to one of yours.
8. Where to read: a link out to buy or borrow, when one exists.

Several of those areas have their own entries in "Ratings you can trust," published with the next part of this guide.
