**What it is.** The rows of books on the homepage: Trending, Community Favorites, Hidden Gems, a row for each major genre, and Recently added.

**What it is based on.** Each shelf is computed from real ratings, and the trust shelves change with the active view. On the Unbnd house view, Trending and Favorites count the readers the site's network trusts; on your view, they count the people you follow. The line above the shelves names which view you are seeing.

**How to use it.**

1. Scroll the homepage; every shelf row slides sideways.
2. Check the "Showing" line near the top to see whose judgment built the shelves.
