**What it is.** The search box at the top of every page. It looks across titles, authors, and the genre and style tags readers have applied.

**What it is based on.** The catalog itself, including books the community added, kept current as ratings and tags come in.

**How to use it.**

1. Type a title, an author, or a genre like "thriller" into the box.
2. Press enter for the full results page, or pick from the suggestions as you type.
3. Open any result to land on its book page.
