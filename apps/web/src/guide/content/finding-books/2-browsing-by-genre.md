**What it is.** Sixteen genres, each with its own page of books. The genre chips on a book page and the Explore genres row on the homepage both lead there.

**What it is based on.** A book's genres come from readers and from the catalog's own classification of its subjects. They are tags anyone can suggest and anyone can dispute, so a book's genre page reflects what readers call it.

**How to use it.**

1. Pick a genre from the Explore genres row on the homepage, or choose Browse in the menu.
2. On any book page, click a genre chip to see its neighbors.
