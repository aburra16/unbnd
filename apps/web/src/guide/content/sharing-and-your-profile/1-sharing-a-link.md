**What it is.** Any book or profile address here can be pasted anywhere people talk. On the platforms that show link previews, a book link turns into a card: the cover, the title and author, the community rating, and the book's top tags.

**Why the card shows the community number.** A preview card is seen by people who have not signed in, so it carries the one rating that is the same for everyone. The trusted views wait for the reader on the page itself.

**What the receiver gets.** The full book page, readable without an account. The person you sent it to sees everything you see, and meets an account question only if they try to rate or save.
