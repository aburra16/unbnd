**What it is.** The card in Settings called "Take ownership of your account." It exists because an email account here works like this: everything you publish is signed with a key, the key to your account, and until you take ownership, the site keeps that key for you, locked with your password. Taking ownership means being shown the key itself, once, so you hold the one thing that makes your account portable and provably yours.

**What you have now.** With an email account, the site holds your key and signs your ratings with it when you act. Your password locks it; the site cannot use it without you.

**What changes when you take ownership.** You see the key one time, copy it, and store it somewhere safe of your choosing. From then on, a copy of your account's proof exists outside this site, in your hands.

**What stays the same.** Everything. Your account keeps working exactly as before; you sign in the same way; nothing about your day here changes.

**What you become responsible for.** The key is the account: anyone holding it can act as you, and a key that leaks cannot be un-leaked. Keep it like the key it is.

**How to use it.**

1. Open Settings and find the card "Take ownership of your account."
2. Read each step; the flow explains itself and asks for your password before showing anything.
3. The key is shown once. Copy it, store it safely, and confirm you have it.
4. The card then shows that ownership is taken, and life continues as normal.
