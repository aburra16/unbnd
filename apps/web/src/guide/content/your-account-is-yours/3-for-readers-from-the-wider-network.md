This note is for readers who already know the nostr world. If that is not you, skip it; nothing else in this guide needs it.

Unbnd is a nostr application. Your ratings, reviews, tags, and shelves are signed nostr events under your own npub, published to the site's relay and readable by any client that speaks the same shapes. "Sign in with Nostr" uses your NIP-07 browser extension; an email account is custodial, with the nsec held encrypted against your password, and "Take ownership of your account" is the nsec export. The trust layer derives from follows, seeded by the site's own curation identity. Bring your existing identity, or take the one you make here anywhere else on the network.
