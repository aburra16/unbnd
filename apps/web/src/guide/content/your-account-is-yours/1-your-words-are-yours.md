**What it is.** Every rating, review, tag, and shelf you make here is published under your own name, as a record you authored. The site displays your words; it cannot quietly rewrite them, and a change to your rating only ever comes from you, signed the same way the original was.

**What that means in practice.** Your reading life is not locked in a company's database. The records are yours in a way that survives the site itself: the same account can carry them to other places that read the same records, and the next entry explains how to hold that power in your own hands.
