**What it is.** The short line that sometimes appears under a book's rating: "Hidden gem" when the trusted view rates the book clearly above the crowd, "Overhyped" when the trusted view is clearly cooler on it. When the two agree, the line says nothing, on purpose.

**What it is based on.** Two averages from the same book: the trusted average for your active view, and the crowd's average. The crowd is every reader with any standing in that view, each counted once, so ratings from accounts with no standing never move the line. The line shows when the gap between the two is wide enough to mean something and a handful of trusted readers stand behind it. One enthusiastic rating never makes a gem.

**How to use it.**

1. Watch for the line under the rating on book pages.
2. Flip the view switch: a hidden gem from the house's vantage may not be one from yours, because the judges changed.
