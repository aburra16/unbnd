**What it is.** The order control on a book's reviews. Most trusted puts the readers with the highest standing in the active view first. Best taste match puts the readers who score books most like you first.

**How to use it.**

1. Open a book with several reviews.
2. Pick the order above the review list. Most trusted is the default; Best taste match needs you signed in, since the match is computed from your ratings.
