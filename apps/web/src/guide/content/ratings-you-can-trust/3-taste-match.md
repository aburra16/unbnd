**What it is.** The percentage on a reader's profile, and beside reviewer names on a book page, telling you how often you and that person score the same books the same way. "87% match · 24 books in common" means that across 24 shared books, your scores mostly agree.

**What it is based on.** Books you have both rated, and how close the two scores sit on each one. Only the overlap of your ratings feeds it; genres and follower counts play no part. It is computed from your side, signed in; two different readers see two different numbers on the same profile.

**The honest floor.** Below five books in common, the chip reads "Not enough overlap yet" instead of showing a percentage built on too little. Rate more books you have both read and the number appears.

**How to use it.**

1. Sign in and rate books; the match needs your ratings to compare against.
2. Open a reader's profile and look under their name.
3. On a book page, the match also sits beside reviewer names, so you can weigh a review by how much its writer reads like you.
