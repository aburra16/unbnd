**What it is.** Following a reader tells the site whose judgment matters to you; it is how your own view gets built. The Followers number on a profile shows how many readers with standing follow that person.

**What it is based on.** Following is your plain choice and takes effect at once. The followers count is deliberately conservative: it comes from the site's trust network rather than a raw headcount, so it reflects followers who are real readers with standing, and it reads "No followers yet" until the network has something honest to report.

**How to use it.**

1. Open a reader's profile and choose Follow.
2. Follow several readers whose taste you respect; your own view of every book sharpens with each one.
3. To stop, open the same profile and choose Unfollow. Your view updates the same way.
