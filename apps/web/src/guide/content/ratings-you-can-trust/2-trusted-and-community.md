**What it is.** Every book shows two kinds of count. Community consensus is the plain tally: every rating and every tag, from everyone, counted once. Trusted ratings are the subset written by readers with standing in the active view.

**What it is based on.** The community numbers are raw arithmetic and never weighted. The trusted numbers weigh each rater by their standing, so one respected reader can outweigh a pile of strangers, and a pile of strangers can never drown a respected reader.

**Where you will see it.** A book with no trusted ratings says so plainly: "No trusted ratings for this book yet." The community tally still shows. Nothing here fakes confidence it does not have; a missing number means the data does not exist yet, and it fills in as trusted readers rate.
