**What it is.** Every book shows two kinds of count. Community consensus is the plain tally: how many ratings and tags it has, from everyone, counted once. Trusted ratings are the ones written by readers with standing in the active view, and only they make the star figure.

**What it is based on.** The community tally is a plain count. The trusted figure weighs each rater by their standing, so one respected reader can outweigh a pile of strangers.

**Where you will see it.** A book with no trusted ratings says so plainly: "No trusted ratings for this book yet." It shows how many ratings it has and the reviews, with no star figure. The figure appears once a trusted reader rates the book.
