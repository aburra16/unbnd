**What it is.** The line near a book's rating that says "Showing: Unbnd house view" names whose judgment built the numbers you are looking at. The house view counts the readers this site's network trusts, the way a good bookshop's staff picks shelf counts the staff. Once you follow enough readers, a switch appears and you can flip to your own view, where the judges are the people you chose.

**What it is based on.** Trust here spreads along follows. The site's own reading identity follows its founding readers; the people those readers follow earn standing too, and so on outward, with standing fading as it travels. A rating counts toward the house view when its writer holds standing in that web.

**How to use it.**

1. Open any book page and read the "Showing" line above the rating.
2. After you follow readers of your own, use the switch beside it to flip between the house view and yours.
3. Watch the average and the review order change with the judges. The books never change; the jury does.
