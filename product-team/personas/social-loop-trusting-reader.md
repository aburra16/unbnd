# Persona: The Trusting Reader

**Slug:** social-loop-trusting-reader
**Priority:** Secondary
**Date:** 2026-06-06

## Who they are

A reader who follows specific people's taste rather than algorithms. They arrive because a curator they respect shared a link or sent an invite. They are worn down by Goodreads and StoryGraph recommendations that feel generic and crowd-sourced. They rate occasionally rather than constantly, and they are not here to do work. They are here because a person they trust pointed at a book.

## What they want

Recommendations that come from specific people whose taste they trust, with the reason visible.

## Their core loop

Land on a curator's shelf or a book → see a taste match and why the book surfaced → rate a few books → receive personalized hidden-gems and for-you results → follow more curators → discovery gets steadily more personal.

## What they won't tolerate

- An account wall before they have seen any value.
- Recommendations that feel like everyone else's.
- Trust numbers with no human behind them.
- Signal that looks fabricated on a thin graph.

## Notes

The first-visit experience is the entire game for this persona. They must understand a curator's taste and find a book worth their time before any account exists. Rating, the natural hook, is also the point where an account is likely required. That tension is the first thing scope and experience must resolve.

Taste match must stay honest on a thin graph. With little co-rating overlap, the product should say "not enough overlap yet" rather than show a hollow number (discovery open question 1). This persona converts to a Founding Curator over time, or stays a light but loyal reader. Either outcome is a win, so the design should not force the heavier path.
