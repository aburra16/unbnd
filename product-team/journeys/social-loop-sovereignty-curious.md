# Journey: The Sovereignty-Curious User

**Slug:** social-loop-sovereignty-curious
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/social-loop-sovereignty-curious.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-06

A single high-stakes moment, not a lifecycle. The trigger is investment, and the stakes are irreversible if mishandled. There is no first-visit step here, because this persona is always an existing custodial user by the time the journey begins.

## Steps

### 1. The nag
- **Trigger:** they have invested real curation, or they are using another nostr app, and they wonder whether they are locked in.
- **Action:** they look for an answer to "can I take this with me?"
- **Expected experience:** an honest, findable statement that yes, they can, and a clear pointer to where.
- **Emotional state:** testing the promise the product makes about itself.

### 2. Understanding the choice
- **Trigger:** they open the sovereignty-upgrade path.
- **Action:** they read what holding their own keys actually means.
- **Expected experience:** a plain-language explanation of the tradeoff, control and portability in exchange for responsibility, with appropriate gravity and no jargon.
- **Emotional state:** cautious, weighing it.

### 3. The export itself
- **Trigger:** they decide to proceed.
- **Action:** they export or claim their key, moving through deliberate confirmation steps.
- **Expected experience:** a flow that is careful but not frightening. They finish understanding that they now hold the key and what that obligates.
- **Emotional state:** empowered if it is handled with the right weight, scared off if it is mishandled in either direction.

### 4. Life after
- **Trigger:** they use their key elsewhere, or simply keep it as insurance.
- **Action:** they sign in to other nostr clients with the same identity, or do nothing and rest easy.
- **Expected experience:** their identity works across nostr, and nothing breaks inside Unbnd.
- **Emotional state:** their trust in the platform deepens, precisely because leaving was made genuinely possible.

## Friction points

- **Wrong level of gravity.** Too scary and they bail. Too casual and it feels unsafe. The calibration is the core design problem (discovery open question 4).
- **Irreversible mistakes.** Losing the exported key is unrecoverable. The flow must guard against a careless loss without burying the user in dread.
- **Pressure to upgrade.** Custodial users must never be made to feel they have to do this. The path is a door that is always open, not a step they are pushed through.
