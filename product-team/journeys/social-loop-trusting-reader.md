# Journey: The Trusting Reader

**Slug:** social-loop-trusting-reader
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/social-loop-trusting-reader.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-06

From first encounter to engaged user. First-visit value is the whole game for this persona.

## Steps

### 1. Arrival via a curator
- **Trigger:** a curator they trust shares a book link or a shelf, or sends an invite.
- **Action:** they click and land on a book detail page or a curator's profile, with no account.
- **Expected experience:** a rich, readable page that shows the curator's take and the trust context around it, fully usable without signing up.
- **Emotional state:** curious, low commitment.

### 2. Sensing the difference
- **Trigger:** they browse the curator's shelf or a book's signals.
- **Action:** they read the trusted-versus-community framing and see the real people behind the ratings.
- **Expected experience:** it reads like a specific person's recommendation, not an algorithm's average.
- **Emotional state:** intrigued. "This is that person's actual taste."

### 3. The hook
- **Trigger:** they want this tailored to themselves.
- **Action:** they rate a few books. This is the point where an account is likely required.
- **Expected experience:** minimal friction to begin, and a quick first reward: a taste match to the curator who brought them in, and a first hidden-gem or for-you result.
- **Emotional state:** pleased if the value is immediate, annoyed if they hit a wall too early.

### 4. Personalization deepens
- **Trigger:** they rate more books and follow more curators.
- **Action:** they build their own small trust graph.
- **Expected experience:** for-you and hidden-gems sharpen, and why-recommended attributes each surfaced book to a specific person they trust.
- **Emotional state:** this is mine now.

### 5. Light regular, or convert to curator
- **Trigger:** they return for trusted discovery.
- **Action:** they keep reading and rating, and may begin curating in earnest.
- **Expected experience:** discovery stays personal and improves as their graph grows.
- **Emotional state:** loyal, whether as a light reader or a new curator.

## Friction points

- **The account wall before value.** Rating requires an account, but this persona must get value before one exists. This is a design problem to resolve in scope and experience, not to accept. It is the first thing the next phases should settle.
- **Thin-graph honesty deflating the hook.** Taste match needs enough co-rating overlap to be honest. "Not enough overlap yet" is correct but can feel like a letdown at exactly the moment the reader is deciding to invest.
- **No felt difference from Goodreads.** If the first contact does not feel meaningfully more personal than the incumbents, they leave and do not come back.
