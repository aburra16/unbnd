# Journey: The Founding Curator

**Slug:** social-loop-founding-curator
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/social-loop-founding-curator.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-06

From first encounter to engaged user. The make-or-break path for Phase 3, because this persona seeds the entire trust graph.

## Steps

### 1. The ask
- **Trigger:** the founder personally asks them to help seed the platform.
- **Action:** they visit the site, possibly before creating any account.
- **Expected experience:** a real catalog and visible existing curation, not an empty shell. A clear, plain-language frame for what curating here gives them, beyond doing a favor.
- **Emotional state:** goodwill toward the founder, mild skepticism ("another book app?").

### 2. First curation session
- **Trigger:** they decide to add a few books they love.
- **Action:** rate, tag, and review books; claim books they authored if they are a writer.
- **Expected experience:** fast, low-friction entry, and an immediate sense that their assertion matters. Their rating shapes a book's consensus and shows on their profile right away.
- **Emotional state:** willing, watching closely for whether this is worth their time.

### 3. The payoff moment
- **Trigger:** they look at a peer's profile or a book detail page.
- **Action:** they notice a taste-match figure to a peer, and a hidden-gem signal on a book.
- **Expected experience:** the app demonstrably knows what they like and who reads like them. The favor starts to feel like something they would use anyway.
- **Emotional state:** the loan of goodwill converts to genuine interest.

### 4. Vouching and growing the circle
- **Trigger:** they know other curators worth bringing in.
- **Action:** they vouch for peers by tagging them as curators, and invite them.
- **Expected experience:** the vouch visibly counts toward that peer's curator status, the graph grows, and their circle becomes legible on the platform.
- **Emotional state:** ownership, a sense of building something rather than filling a database.

### 5. Becoming a regular
- **Trigger:** they return over days and weeks.
- **Action:** routine rating and reviewing; they share book and shelf links outward.
- **Expected experience:** their curation travels as rich, unfurling cards; their reputation accrues as portable credit they could take elsewhere.
- **Emotional state:** invested. This is now theirs too, not only a favor to a friend.

## Friction points

- **The empty-graph void.** Early on, their ratings have few peers to match against, so the payoff that would hook them (step 3) is at its thinnest exactly when they are deciding whether to stay. This is the cold-start tension and the central risk of the whole phase.
- **Honest-empty feeling lifeless.** The no-fake-data rule means taste match and hidden gems fall back to honest-empty states on a thin graph. Correct, but it can read as a dead app if not handled with care.
- **The free-data-entry feeling.** If personal value lags behind the effort of rating and tagging, the relational goodwill runs out and they drift.
- **A vouch that does nothing.** If vouching for a peer has no visible effect, the growth mechanism that expands the graph stalls.
