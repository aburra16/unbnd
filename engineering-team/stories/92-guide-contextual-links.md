# Story 92: Contextual entry points

**Status:** Planning
**Created:** 2026-06-12
**Type:** Feature (web; the epic's last story)
**Source brief:** `product-team/stories-queue.md` Story 10 · **PRD:** §5.5 · **Design rule:** the quietest existing affordance per surface; one click to the anchor; never a modal or tooltip essay.

## Background
The guide is complete (36 entries, every anchor stable); confusion can now route to its answer at the moment it happens. Seven listed surfaces gain a one-click door to their matching entry: the House/Yours switch, taste-match chips, the hype-gap line, vouch counts, contested tags, reviewed-flag rows, and the "Removal queued" state.

## Acceptance criteria
From the brief: each listed surface routes to its entry's anchor in one click; the quietest existing affordance per surface; never a modal or tooltip essay; the affordances render sensibly signed out where the surface itself does; no surface gained visual weight (the design system's existing treatments only).
